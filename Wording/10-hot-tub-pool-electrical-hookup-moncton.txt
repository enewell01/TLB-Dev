INTRO
Hot tubs and pools mix water and electricity, which makes the hookup one of the least forgiving jobs in residential electrical work. TLB Electric installs dedicated circuits, proper GFCI protection, and code-required disconnects for both.

WHAT TO EXPECT
A dedicated circuit sized to your hot tub or pool pump, with a GFCI breaker and disconnect at the code-required distance and bonding of metal components. Covers outdoor-rated wiring and conduit for exterior or underground runs, matched to your installer's specifications.

BODY COPY
A hot tub or pool hookup has specific code requirements that a general-purpose outlet can't satisfy: a dedicated circuit sized correctly for the pump and heater load, GFCI protection, and an emergency disconnect installed within sight of the equipment but at a safe distance from the water. TLB Electric follows those requirements exactly, not as an afterthought but as the starting point for the whole job.

Every hot tub and pool comes with its own manufacturer electrical specifications, and we coordinate directly with those requirements, and with your installer if one is involved, so the circuit, breaker, and disconnect all match what the equipment actually needs. Getting this wrong doesn't just risk a shock hazard; it can also void the equipment's warranty.

Whether it's a new hot tub going onto an existing deck, an in-ground pool being wired during construction, or an older hookup that was never brought up to current code, we handle the electrical side so the rest of the installation can move forward with a safe, compliant connection already in place.
