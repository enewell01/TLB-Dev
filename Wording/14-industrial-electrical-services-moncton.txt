Industrial facilities put different demands on an electrical system than a typical commercial space: higher loads, three-phase power, and equipment that can't tolerate unplanned outages. TLB Electric sizes and installs industrial electrical work to match how the equipment is actually used, not a generic commercial spec.

We handle new equipment hookups, panel and distribution work, and troubleshooting for facilities where downtime has a real cost. Work is scheduled around your production or shift schedule wherever possible, so an electrical job doesn't turn into lost output.
