INTRO
Adding EV charging to a condo or apartment building means navigating shared electrical service, board approval, and metering questions before a single charger goes in. TLB Electric handles the electrical side, from one resident's parking spot to a planned bank of chargers.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Charger installation in condo and multi-unit buildings, starting with an assessment of the shared service and available capacity. Covers individual unit chargers with metering options, load management for buildings adding chargers over time, and permits for shared-property work.

BODY COPY
Multi-unit charging is as much a planning problem as an electrical one. Most older buildings weren't designed with EV charging in mind, so the first step is understanding what the building's existing service can actually support before recommending a metering or charging setup.

We work with whoever owns the decision, whether that is a condo board, a property manager, or an individual owner, to install charging that fits the building's realistic capacity today and can expand as more residents want a charger, rather than a one-off install that boxes out the next request.
