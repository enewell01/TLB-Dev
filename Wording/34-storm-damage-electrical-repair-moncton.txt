INTRO
High wind and winter storms can take out more than just the power: a damaged weatherhead, a torn-loose service mast, or a cracked meter base needs a licensed electrician before the utility will safely reconnect service. TLB Electric assesses and repairs storm damage to get service restored properly, not just patched.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Assessment and repair of storm damage to the weatherhead, service mast, and meter base, including wind and fallen-tree damage to the service entrance. We coordinate with the utility on reconnection requirements, arrange temporary power where needed, and document the work for insurance claims.

BODY COPY
When a storm damages the point where power enters a home, meaning the weatherhead, the mast, or the meter base, the utility generally won't reconnect until that damage is repaired and inspected, which leaves a household without power on top of the original problem. TLB Electric prioritizes these repairs specifically because they're the blocker standing between you and getting reconnected.

We handle the repair and the coordination with the utility so the process moves as fast as it reasonably can, and we document the damage clearly in case it's needed for an insurance claim tied to the storm.
