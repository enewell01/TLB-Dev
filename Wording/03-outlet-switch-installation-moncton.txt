BODY COPY
Adding an outlet sounds like a small job, but it's one of the most common ways homeowners end up with an overloaded circuit or an improperly wired connection. TLB Electric runs the wiring properly the first time: matching wire gauge to breaker size, grounding correctly, and confirming the circuit has room for the added load before anything gets connected.

Switches and outlets also wear out. A switch that feels loose, sparks, or has stopped working cleanly is often a sign of a failing connection behind the wall, not just the device itself. We check the wiring at the box, not just the faceplate, so the fix actually lasts.

This is also where a lot of small upgrades pay off quickly: GFCI protection in a kitchen or bathroom that never had it, a dimmer swapped in for better control over a room, or a dead outlet finally brought back to life. These are quick jobs for a licensed electrician and they close real safety gaps in the process.
