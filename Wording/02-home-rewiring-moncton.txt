INTRO
If your home's wiring is original to the house, undersized for modern appliances, or showing warning signs like warm outlets and flickering lights, a full or partial rewire from TLB Electric brings the system up to current safety standards.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Full or partial rewiring, replacing outdated cloth, aluminum, or degraded copper wiring with circuits sized for modern kitchen, laundry, and electronics loads. Permits and inspection are handled, wall and ceiling patching is coordinated, and the panel is labeled with a circuit map at completion.

LOCAL NOTE
A large share of the housing stock in Moncton, Dieppe, and Riverview predates modern electrical code, and we regularly rewire homes where the original wiring was never designed for today's electrical loads.

BODY COPY
Rewiring a home is disruptive by nature, so TLB Electric plans the work to minimize the impact on your household. That usually means staging the job room by room, working around lived-in spaces, and coordinating closely with any drywall or renovation contractor already on site. You'll know upfront what's being replaced, why, and what to expect each day.

Not every home needs a full rewire. Sometimes the issue is isolated to one circuit, one addition, or one section of knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring that was never brought up to standard. We inspect the whole system first and recommend only the scope that's actually needed, whether that is a full rewire, a partial rewire, or targeted circuit replacement, rather than defaulting to the most expensive option.

Old wiring isn't just an inconvenience; it's a fire and shock risk, and it can also affect your ability to get home insurance or a mortgage renewal. A rewire performed to current code resolves both problems at once: a safer home, and paperwork that satisfies your insurer's requirements.
