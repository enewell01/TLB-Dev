INTRO
A detached garage or workshop needs its own properly sized service, not an extension cord run from the house. TLB Electric wires new and existing outbuildings for lighting, outlets, and heavier tools, run correctly from day one.

WHAT TO EXPECT
Power to detached garages and workshops, including a new subpanel or feed run underground or overhead. Circuits are sized for the equipment you actually run, such as welders and compressors, with lighting and outlet layout planned around how the space is used.

BODY COPY
An outbuilding's electrical needs usually outgrow whatever got run to it originally, especially once real shop equipment shows up: a welder, a compressor, a table saw drawing more than a single 15-amp circuit was ever meant to carry. TLB Electric sizes the feed and sub-panel around the equipment you actually plan to run, not just lighting and a couple of outlets.

Whether it's a new detached garage going up alongside a house build or an existing workshop that's outgrown its wiring, we plan the underground or overhead feed, the sub-panel, and the circuit layout together so the space is genuinely usable, not just technically wired.
