BODY COPY
A circuit breaker that trips repeatedly under a normal load, won't stay reset, or feels warm at the panel is a sign that it, or the circuit behind it, needs attention. TLB Electric tests the breaker and the circuit it protects to find out whether the fix is a straightforward breaker swap or something further down the line.

Every panel brand uses its own breaker style, and mismatched or off-brand breakers are a common problem we find in older installations. We source and install breakers properly matched to your panel, including arc-fault and ground-fault breakers for circuits that now require them under current code, such as bedrooms, kitchens, and bathrooms.

Some older panel brands, including Federal Pioneer, use breaker styles that are becoming harder to source or that carry known reliability issues of their own. In those cases, we'll tell you plainly whether a one-off breaker replacement makes sense or a full panel replacement is the more practical long-term fix. The goal is a solution that holds up, not just a quick patch.
