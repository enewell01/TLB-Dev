Multi-Unit & Condo EV Charging Installation

META DESCRIPTION
EV charger installation for condos, apartments, and multi-unit properties in Moncton, NB. Licensed work coordinated with boards and property managers.

INTRO
Adding EV charging to a condo or apartment building means navigating shared electrical service, board approval, and metering questions before a single charger goes in. TLB Electric handles the electrical side, from one resident's parking spot to a planned bank of chargers.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Assessment of the building's shared electrical service and available capacity
- Individual unit charger installation with dedicated metering options
- Coordination with condo boards, property managers, and building owners
- Load management planning for buildings adding chargers over time
- Permit and inspection handling for shared-property electrical work

BODY COPY
Multi-unit charging is as much a planning problem as an electrical one. Most older buildings weren't designed with EV charging in mind, so the first step is understanding what the building's existing service can actually support before recommending a metering or charging setup.

We work with whoever owns the decision — a condo board, a property manager, or an individual owner — to install charging that fits the building's realistic capacity today and can expand as more residents want a charger, rather than a one-off install that boxes out the next request.
