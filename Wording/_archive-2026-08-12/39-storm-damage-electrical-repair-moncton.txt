Storm & Weather Damage Electrical Repair

META DESCRIPTION
Storm-damaged service line, weatherhead, or meter base in Moncton, Dieppe & Riverview, NB? Licensed emergency repair from TLB Electric. Call now.

INTRO
High wind and winter storms can take out more than just the power — a damaged weatherhead, a torn-loose service mast, or a cracked meter base needs a licensed electrician before the utility will safely reconnect service. TLB Electric assesses and repairs storm damage to get service restored properly, not just patched.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Assessment of weatherhead, service mast, and meter base storm damage
- Coordination with the utility on reconnection requirements after repair
- Temporary power solutions while permanent repairs are scheduled
- Repair of wind and fallen-tree related service entrance damage
- Documentation suitable for insurance claims related to storm damage

BODY COPY
When a storm damages the point where power enters a home — the weatherhead, the mast, the meter base — the utility generally won't reconnect until that damage is repaired and inspected, which leaves a household without power on top of the original problem. TLB Electric prioritizes these repairs specifically because they're the blocker standing between you and getting reconnected.

We handle the repair and the coordination with the utility so the process moves as fast as it reasonably can, and we document the damage clearly in case it's needed for an insurance claim tied to the storm.
