EV Charger Repair & Troubleshooting

META DESCRIPTION
EV charger not charging, tripping breakers, or showing a fault code? Licensed diagnosis and repair in Moncton, Dieppe & Riverview, NB. Free quotes.

INTRO
A home or commercial EV charger that won't power on, won't charge fully, or trips a breaker usually points to a wiring, connection, or circuit problem rather than the charger itself. TLB Electric diagnoses and repairs EV charger installations, whether we installed them or not.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Diagnosis of chargers that won't power on, won't charge fully, or trip breakers
- Repair of loose connections, damaged wiring, and failed circuits feeding the charger
- Troubleshooting third-party or builder-installed chargers we didn't originally install
- Load reassessment if the charger has never run reliably at full speed
- Clear diagnosis before any repair, so you know what's actually wrong first

BODY COPY
Charger problems that look like a defective unit are, more often than not, a circuit that was undersized from the start or a connection that's worked itself loose. TLB Electric starts every repair call with a proper diagnosis at the panel and the charger's dedicated circuit, not a guess based on the charger's brand or age.

If the charger was installed by another contractor or came with a new build, we're glad to take on the repair without requiring a full reinstall. In some cases the fix is a simple connection repair; in others, a charger that's never run at full speed reveals a panel that was already close to its limit and needs a closer look.
