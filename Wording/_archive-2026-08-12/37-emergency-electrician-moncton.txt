BODY COPY
Southeastern New Brunswick's winter storms and wind events knock out power and damage service connections every season, and not every outage is a utility problem — sometimes it's a failing panel, a damaged weatherhead, or a connection that's arced. TLB Electric can tell the difference quickly and make the call on whether it's safe to wait or needs to be fixed now.

Not every after-hours call is an emergency, and we'll tell you honestly if something can safely wait for a regular appointment. But for the situations that can't — a breaker panel that's hot to the touch, sparking outlets, or a total loss of power with no utility outage reported — we respond as an emergency, with pricing explained before any work starts.
