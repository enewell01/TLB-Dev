EV Charger Panel & Circuit Upgrades

META DESCRIPTION
Panel too small for an EV charger? TLB Electric sizes and upgrades your panel and circuit in Moncton, Dieppe & Riverview, NB. Free quotes.

INTRO
Most panel work tied to an EV charger isn't a separate project — it's the first step of the charger installation itself, sized specifically around what the charger needs alongside everything else already running in the home.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Load calculation specific to Level 2 EV charger circuits
- Panel capacity upgrades sized for the charger plus existing household load
- Dedicated circuit sizing and breaker selection for the charger
- Sub-panel installation where a full panel swap isn't necessary
- Coordination with the charger installation so it's one project, not two

BODY COPY
A charger that trips the main breaker or can't run at full speed almost always comes back to the panel, not the charger. TLB Electric runs the load calculation before recommending anything, so you know whether your existing service can take the added draw or whether an upgrade should happen first.

Where a full 200-amp service isn't needed, a smaller sub-panel dedicated to the charger circuit can solve the problem without the cost of replacing the whole system. We'll walk through both options and recommend what actually fits your home's panel and your electrician's assessment of its condition.
