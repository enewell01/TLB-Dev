INTRO
Charging an EV at home starts with the right circuit, not just a plug near the driveway. TLB Electric installs Level 2 chargers sized to your panel's actual capacity, so the charger runs at full speed without tripping breakers or overloading your service.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

- Level 2 (240V) charger installation for all major EV brands
- Dedicated circuit wiring from panel to garage or driveway
- Load calculations to confirm your panel can handle the added draw
- Permit and inspection coordination
- Panel upgrade recommendation when the existing service is undersized

LOCAL NOTE
We install home EV chargers in garages and driveways across Moncton, Dieppe, and Riverview, including older homes where the existing panel needs a closer look before a charger is added.

BODY COPY
A dedicated 240V circuit is the difference between a charger that tops up your car overnight and one that trips your main breaker the first time the dryer runs at the same time. TLB Electric starts every EV charger job with a load calculation on your existing panel — not a guess — so you know upfront whether your service can take the extra draw or whether a panel upgrade should happen first.

We install chargers for all the common EV brands, in attached and detached garages, and on exterior walls for driveway parking. Where a charger is going into an older home, we treat the circuit and the panel as one project, not two separate calls, so you're not paying for a return visit once the charger reveals a panel that was already close to its limit.
