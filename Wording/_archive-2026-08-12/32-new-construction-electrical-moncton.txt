META DESCRIPTION
New construction electrical rough-in for homes, additions, and commercial builds in Moncton, NB. Licensed wiring from TLB Electric, from rough-in to trim-out.

INTRO
New construction wiring is easiest and cheapest to get right before the drywall goes up. TLB Electric works directly with builders, general contractors, and owner-builders on rough-in electrical for new homes, additions, and commercial builds across the Moncton area.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Rough-in wiring for new residential builds
- Panel and service sizing matched to the finished home's load
- Coordination with builders and general contractors on scheduling
- Layout planning for outlets, switches, and lighting circuits
- Rough-in inspection prep and code compliance
- Final trim-out once drywall and finishing are complete

BODY COPY
Rough-in is where the electrical plan actually gets set — where outlets land, how many circuits a kitchen gets, whether the panel is sized for a future EV charger or hot tub instead of just what's in the plans today. TLB Electric works from the builder's drawings but flags gaps early, before a wall is closed up and a change means cutting drywall back open.

We're used to working alongside general contractors and other trades on a build schedule, showing up for rough-in, passing inspection, and returning for trim-out once the space is finished. For owner-builders managing their own trades, we'll walk the site with you before rough-in starts so the panel and circuit layout matches how you actually plan to use the space.
