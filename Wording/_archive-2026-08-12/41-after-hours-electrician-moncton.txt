After-Hours Electrical Service

META DESCRIPTION
Need an electrician evenings or weekends but it's not a true emergency? TLB Electric offers after-hours appointments in Moncton, Dieppe & Riverview, NB.

INTRO
Not every electrical job that comes up outside 9-to-5 is an emergency — sometimes it's just the only time that works. TLB Electric books after-hours and weekend appointments for real, non-urgent electrical work, separate from our true emergency response.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Scheduled evening and weekend appointments for non-emergency work
- Same licensed, code-compliant work as our regular daytime jobs
- Clear scheduling so you're not waiting in an all-day window
- Honest guidance on whether your issue is a true emergency or can be scheduled
- Available for both residential and commercial after-hours requests

BODY COPY
Plenty of real electrical work doesn't fit inside a workday — a homeowner who's only free evenings, a business that can't have an electrician on site during operating hours, a landlord coordinating around a tenant's schedule. TLB Electric books after-hours appointments for that work directly, rather than treating everything outside 9-to-5 as an emergency call-out.

If you're not sure whether what you're dealing with is genuinely urgent or can wait for a scheduled after-hours visit, tell us what's going on and we'll give you a straight answer — we'd rather book you in properly than have you pay emergency rates for something that didn't need them.
