META DESCRIPTION
Commercial EV charger installation for offices, retail, and multi-unit properties in Moncton, NB. Licensed electrical work sized to your building's service. Free quotes.

INTRO
Adding EV charging for staff, tenants, or customers is an electrical infrastructure project, not a one-off outlet swap. TLB Electric plans and installs commercial charging stations sized to your building's existing service and future expansion plans.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Site assessment of existing electrical service and available capacity
- Single and multi-port Level 2 commercial charger installation
- Dedicated circuits and sub-panel work for charging banks
- Coordination with property owners and property managers on permits
- Load management planning for multi-unit or fleet charging
- Ongoing service and troubleshooting for installed stations

LOCAL NOTE
We've quoted and installed charging infrastructure for offices, retail properties, and multi-unit buildings across the greater Moncton area, where most commercial services weren't originally designed with EV charging in mind.

BODY COPY
Most commercial buildings around Moncton were built before EV charging was a consideration, which means the first question isn't which charger to buy — it's whether the building's electrical service has the capacity to run it. TLB Electric assesses your existing service, panel, and distribution before recommending a charging setup, so you're not stuck redoing the work in two years when demand grows.

For property owners planning ahead for multiple charging stations, we design with load management in mind: staggering charge cycles, sizing sub-panels for future ports, and coordinating with landlords or condo boards on permits and approvals. Whether it's a single charger for an office fleet vehicle or a bank of ports for tenant parking, the work is scoped to what your building can actually support.
