Commercial Emergency Electrical Repair

META DESCRIPTION
24/7 emergency electrician for businesses in Moncton, Dieppe & Riverview, NB. Fast response for power loss, tripped panels, and equipment downtime.

INTRO
A power outage or electrical fault at a business carries a different cost than at a home — lost sales, spoiled inventory, or a production line down. TLB Electric prioritizes commercial emergency calls and works to get a business back up safely, fast.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Priority emergency response for retail, office, and industrial properties
- Diagnosis of building-wide or single-tenant power loss
- Emergency repair coordinated with property managers and landlords
- After-hours response for businesses that can't wait for a next-day appointment
- Clear pricing communicated before work begins, even outside business hours

BODY COPY
A residential emergency call and a commercial one aren't the same job. A business calling with no power needs someone who can talk to a property manager, understand a multi-tenant panel, and prioritize getting critical circuits — refrigeration, security, point-of-sale — back online first if a full repair will take longer.

We treat commercial emergency calls as exactly that: a priority, not a same-day-if-we-get-to-it request. You'll get a clear price before work starts and a straight answer about what's actually causing the outage, even outside regular business hours.
