Deck, Patio & Outdoor Living Electrical

META DESCRIPTION
Outdoor lighting, outlets, and wiring for decks, patios, and outdoor kitchens in Moncton, Dieppe & Riverview, NB. Licensed electrician. Free quotes.

INTRO
An outdoor living space adds electrical demands most homes weren't originally wired for — string lighting, an outdoor kitchen, a fire feature, or a heater all need outlets and circuits rated for outside, not just extended from an interior plug.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Weatherproof outlet and GFCI installation for decks and patios
- Outdoor and landscape lighting circuits
- Wiring for outdoor kitchens, fire features, and heaters
- Underground and exterior-rated conduit runs
- Coordination with deck builders and landscapers on timing

BODY COPY
Outdoor spaces get treated as an afterthought in a lot of builds, which usually means whatever electrical is out there was run for a single porch light rather than the deck, kitchen, and lighting plan that eventually gets added. TLB Electric plans outdoor circuits for what the space is actually going to become, not just what's there on day one.

Every outdoor circuit runs through weatherproof, exterior-rated components — GFCI protection, sealed boxes, buried or properly routed conduit — so the wiring holds up through a full New Brunswick winter and summer, not just the first season.
