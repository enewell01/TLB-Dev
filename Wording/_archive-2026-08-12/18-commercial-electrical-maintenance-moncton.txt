BODY COPY
Reactive electrical repairs cost more than planned maintenance, and unplanned downtime costs more than either. TLB Electric works with business owners and property managers to set up a maintenance schedule that catches wear, corrosion, and loose connections before they become a service call.

For multi-tenant buildings, we can coordinate maintenance across common areas and individual units, keeping a documented history that's useful for insurance, lease compliance, and future renovation planning.

When something does fail, we prioritize commercial clients for fast diagnosis and repair, because a dead circuit in a retail unit or office isn't something that can wait a week.
