Charger problems that look like a defective unit are, more often than not, a circuit that was undersized from the start or a connection that's worked itself loose. TLB Electric starts every repair call with a proper diagnosis at the panel and the charger's dedicated circuit, not a guess based on the charger's brand or age.

If the charger was installed by another contractor or came with a new build, we're glad to take on the repair without requiring a full reinstall. In some cases the fix is a simple connection repair; in others, a charger that's never run at full speed reveals a panel that was already close to its limit and needs a closer look.
