Older commercial lighting such as fluorescent tubes, metal halide high-bays, and incandescent fixtures costs more to run and more to maintain than modern LED lighting. A retrofit swaps out inefficient fixtures for LED equivalents, often without needing to rework the underlying wiring, which keeps the project cost and disruption low.

Beyond the direct energy savings, LED retrofits typically mean fewer burnt-out lamps, less maintenance callback, and better, more consistent light quality on the sales floor, in the warehouse, or in the office.

Depending on your facility and the scope of the retrofit, your project may qualify for NB Power energy-efficiency incentive programs. Program details and eligibility change over time, so we recommend confirming current terms directly with NB Power as part of your project planning. We're glad to help point you in the right direction.
