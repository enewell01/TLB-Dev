Lighting changes the feel of a room more than almost any other renovation, and it's one of the easiest upgrades to get wrong if the wiring behind it isn't done properly. TLB Electric handles the layout planning for pot lights and multi-fixture installs so the spacing and switching actually make sense in the room, not just on paper.

A lot of lighting calls start with a symptom, such as a fixture that flickers, buzzes, or has simply stopped working, rather than a planned upgrade. We trace those issues back to the actual cause, whether that's a loose connection, a failing dimmer, or a fixture that was never rated for the space it's in, and fix the root problem instead of just swapping the bulb.

For anyone updating a kitchen, finishing a basement, or refreshing a living space, we can also handle dimmer and smart control installation so the new lighting is easy to adjust for the room's actual use, not just switched fully on or off.
