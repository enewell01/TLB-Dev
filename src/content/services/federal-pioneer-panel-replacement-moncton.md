Federal Pioneer is a panel brand that was common in homes built through the 1960s to 1980s. It's now considered an older panel type, and many licensed electricians and insurance providers recommend replacing it as part of routine electrical upgrades, a home sale, or an insurance renewal. If you're not sure what brand of panel is in your home, TLB Electric can identify it and explain your options during a free assessment.

A Federal Pioneer replacement isn't just a swap of the panel box. It's an opportunity to right-size your electrical service for how your home is actually used today. TLB Electric maps every existing circuit, replaces the panel with a modern unit from a current manufacturer, and re-terminates every wire to today's standards. We handle the permit and inspection process from start to finish, so the job is fully documented and code-compliant when it's done.

Because a Federal Pioneer panel is often flagged during a home inspection, a real estate transaction, or an insurance review, we work with homeowners on a timeline that fits. Some clients want it addressed as part of a broader panel upgrade, and others simply want the panel itself replaced. Either way, we walk you through what we find and what we recommend before any work begins.
