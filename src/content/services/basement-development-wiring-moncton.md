A basement development is one of the few renovations where the electrical scope is easy to underestimate, because the empty space rarely hints at what's about to draw power once it's finished: a media room, a second kitchen, a home gym, sometimes a full legal bedroom. We check the panel's actual capacity against the finished plan before running a single new circuit.

If the development includes a bedroom, code has specific requirements around egress, outlet spacing, and interconnected smoke and CO detection that are easy to miss until an inspection catches them. We build those requirements into the rough-in from the start, working around your contractor's framing and drywall schedule rather than as an afterthought.
