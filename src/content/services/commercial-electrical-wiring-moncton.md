Commercial wiring is a different job than residential wiring. It has to account for higher loads, longer runs, tenant separation, and code requirements specific to commercial occupancy classes. TLB Electric plans every commercial wiring project around how the space will actually be used, not just the minimum required to pass inspection.

We work directly with business owners, general contractors, and property managers to keep wiring projects on schedule, whether that's a single tenant fit-up or a full rewire of an existing building. All work is permitted, inspected, and documented so you have a clear record of what was installed.

If you're planning equipment upgrades, additional circuits, or a buildout that involves new electrical service, we can scope the wiring alongside your other trades to avoid costly rework later.
