A charger that trips the main breaker or can't run at full speed almost always comes back to the panel, not the charger. TLB Electric runs the load calculation before recommending anything, so you know whether your existing service can take the added draw or whether an upgrade should happen first.

Where a full 200-amp service isn't needed, a smaller sub-panel dedicated to the charger circuit can solve the problem without the cost of replacing the whole system. We'll walk through both options and recommend what actually fits your home's panel and your electrician's assessment of its condition.
