A dedicated 240V circuit is the difference between a charger that tops up your car overnight and one that trips your main breaker the first time the dryer runs at the same time. TLB Electric starts every EV charger job with a load calculation on your existing panel, not a guess, so you know upfront whether your service can take the extra draw or whether a panel upgrade should happen first.

We install chargers for all the common EV brands, in attached and detached garages, and on exterior walls for driveway parking. Where a charger is going into an older home, we treat the circuit and the panel as one project, not two separate calls, so you're not paying for a return visit once the charger reveals a panel that was already close to its limit.
