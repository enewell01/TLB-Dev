Electrical code compliance issues have a way of surfacing at the worst time, during a property sale, an insurance audit, or a fire inspection. TLB Electric inspects commercial electrical systems proactively, so deficiencies are identified and documented before they become someone else's emergency.

Our inspection covers panels, wiring, grounding, and life-safety systems like emergency lighting and exit signage, checked against current Canadian Electrical Code requirements. You get a written report that spells out exactly what was found and what it would take to correct it.

If corrections are needed, we can complete the work directly and coordinate with the local inspection authority, so you have a clear, closed-out compliance record rather than an open list of concerns.
