Lighting shapes how customers experience a retail space and how comfortably employees work in an office. TLB Electric plans lighting layouts around the actual function of the space, whether that is the sales floor, back office, reception, or storage, rather than installing a uniform grid that doesn't serve any of them well.

For retail buildouts, we install accent and display lighting alongside general lighting so merchandise and signage get the attention they need. For offices, we focus on even, glare-free lighting that meets workplace standards without unnecessary energy waste.

All installations meet current electrical code, including required emergency and exit lighting, and are wired to support future changes as your space evolves.
