A residential emergency call and a commercial one aren't the same job. A business calling with no power needs someone who can talk to a property manager, understand a multi-tenant panel, and prioritize getting critical circuits such as refrigeration, security, and point-of-sale back online first if a full repair will take longer.

We treat commercial emergency calls as exactly that: a priority, not a same-day-if-we-get-to-it request. You'll get a clear price before work starts and a straight answer about what's actually causing the outage, even outside regular business hours.
